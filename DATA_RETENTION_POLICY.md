# 3. User Requests

Where legally applicable, users may request:

- data access
- correction
- deletion
- export of eligible information

Certain archival or governance records may be exempt from deletion where continuity obligations apply.

---

# 4. Secure Storage

We implement reasonable safeguards including:

- encrypted systems
- access controls
- audit logging
- permission restrictions

---

# 5. Deletion Procedures

Data scheduled for deletion may be:

- permanently removed
- anonymized
- archived in restricted systems
- retained where legally required
