# Governance Framework

## Purpose

The governance framework establishes the operational, ethical, safety, and continuity standards for the Bridge Builders Collective platform.

The objective of governance is to ensure:
- participant safety
- operational accountability
- continuity of stewardship
- ethical infrastructure development
- transparent moderation systems
- institutional trust
- long-term resilience

---

# Core Governance Principles

## 1. Stewardship Over Extraction

The platform prioritizes long-term participant wellbeing, continuity, and preservation over short-term engagement extraction.

All systems should support:
- trust
- clarity
- emotional safety
- informed participation
- sustainable interaction

---

## 2. Transparency

Governance systems should remain visible, understandable, and reviewable.

This includes:
- moderation systems
- escalation procedures
- consent systems
- operational responsibilities
- data handling policies
- archive ownership structures

---

## 3. Consent and Safety

Participant consent is foundational to all interactions.

Core requirements include:
- informed consent
- participant control
- revocable participation
- media permissions
- escalation protections
- incident reporting systems

Safety systems must prioritize:
- de-escalation
- emotional stability
- accessibility
- participant dignity

---

## 4. Continuity and Preservation

The platform is designed for long-term continuity.

Governance responsibilities include:
- archive integrity
- metadata preservation
- continuity planning
- succession planning
- documentation versioning
- operational redundancy

---

# Governance Structure

## Foundational Roles

### Founders
Responsible for:
- platform direction
- infrastructure oversight
- governance continuity
- strategic decision-making

### Stewards
Responsible for:
- participant support
- moderation assistance
- onboarding guidance
- operational continuity
- safety observation

### Advisory Participants
May assist with:
- ethics review
- accessibility review
- continuity strategy
- governance recommendations
- institutional partnerships

---

# Moderation and Escalation

Moderation systems are intended to:
- preserve participant safety
- maintain operational integrity
- reduce harm
- support respectful interaction

Escalation systems include:
- incident reporting
- review workflows
- steward escalation procedures
- participant protection protocols
- documentation logging

Detailed procedures are maintained separately in:
- MODERATION_ESCALATION.md
- CONSENT_SYSTEMS.md

---

# Data Governance

The platform prioritizes:
- minimal necessary data collection
- participant ownership awareness
- secure storage systems
- controlled access permissions
- institutional-grade protection standards

Future policies include:
- retention schedules
- deletion request procedures
- archive portability
- media licensing governance

---

# Operational Standards

Operational governance requires:
- documentation consistency
- version tracking
- audit visibility
- continuity planning
- accessibility compliance
- security review procedures

The long-term objective is to create a governance infrastructure capable of supporting:
- intergenerational archive continuity

- ethical cultural preservation
