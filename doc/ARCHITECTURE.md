# Platform Architecture

## Overview

Bridge Builders Collective is designed as a modular human-regulation and continuity infrastructure platform integrating cultural preservation, governance systems, stewardship operations, participant onboarding, and archive continuity.

The platform architecture prioritizes:
- operational trust
- accessibility
- emotional clarity
- scalability
- governance transparency
- long-term continuity
- institutional-grade resilience

---

# Core Architecture Layers

## 1. Frontend Experience Layer

Primary responsibilities:
- onboarding flows
- member interaction
- Living Archive experience
- steward dashboards
- accessibility systems
- governance visibility
- H-ROI visualization
- consent and safety interfaces

Primary stack:
- Lovable
- React
- Node.js

---

## 2. Backend Infrastructure Layer

Primary responsibilities:
- authentication
- authorization
- database systems
- Row Level Security policies
- media storage
- archive continuity systems
- moderation infrastructure
- analytics and metrics

Primary stack:
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Edge Functions

---

## 3. Governance Infrastructure Layer

Responsible for:
- steward operational doctrine
- escalation systems
- moderation protocols
- consent enforcement
- audit logging
- continuity governance
- institutional accountability

Documentation stored in:
- GOVERNANCE.md
- CONSENT_SYSTEMS.md
- MODERATION_ESCALATION.md

---

## 4. Archive Continuity Layer

Responsible for:
- media preservation
- lineage continuity
- intergenerational archive systems
- metadata integrity
- historical preservation workflows
- participant ownership structures

Future expansion includes:
- memory mapping
- timeline reconstruction
- AI-assisted archive navigation
- family continuity systems

---

## 5. Metrics and H-ROI Layer

Responsible for:
- participant outcome measurement
- engagement metrics
- retention analytics
- continuity tracking
- stewardship metrics
- Human Return on Investment (H-ROI)

---

# Design Principles

The platform is designed around:
- calm interaction systems
- low cognitive overload
- trust-centered UX
- continuity over extraction
- accessibility-first interaction
- modular long-term scalability
- institutional-grade documentation and governance

---

# Operational Goal

The long-term objective is to establish a resilient platform capable of supporting:
- community resilience systems
- cultural preservation systems
- educational infrastructure
- archive continuity
- stewardship coordination
- human regulation environments
- measurable social impact systems
