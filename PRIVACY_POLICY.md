# Privacy Policy

## Purpose

Bridge Builders Collective is committed to protecting participant privacy, preserving data integrity, and maintaining transparent stewardship practices.

This policy defines how information is collected, stored, used, protected, and governed across all platform systems.

---

# Data Collected

The platform may collect:

- account information
- participant-submitted content
- archive materials
- onboarding responses
- consent acknowledgements
- accessibility preferences
- voluntary survey responses
- session participation metrics
- uploaded media
- operational analytics

Sensitive personal information is minimized whenever possible.

---

# Consent

Participation requires informed consent.

Users must understand:
- what information is collected
- why it is collected
- how it is used
- how it is stored
- how it may be removed

Consent must be:
- explicit
- revocable
- documented
- understandable

---

# Data Usage

Information is used solely for:

- operational continuity
- archive preservation
- participant support
- system improvement
- safety monitoring
- stewardship operations
- accessibility optimization
- measurable outcomes reporting

The platform does not sell personal data.

---

# Data Protection

Security measures include:

- encrypted infrastructure
- role-based access control
- audit logging
- restricted administrator permissions
- protected storage systems
- secure authentication workflows

---

# Archive Stewardship

Living archive materials are treated as preservation assets, not extractive content assets.

Participants maintain rights over:
- identity representation
- family memory materials
- withdrawal requests
- visibility permissions
- licensing permissions

---

# Data Retention

Data retention periods are defined within the Data Retention Policy.

Expired or revoked materials are removed according to stewardship procedures and legal requirements.

---

# Accessibility

Privacy information must remain:
- readable
- understandable
- accessible across devices
- available in plain language

---

# Policy Updates

Policy revisions are version-tracked and logged publicly where appropriate.

Major revisions require operational review.
