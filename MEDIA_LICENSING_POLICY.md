# Media Licensing Policy

## Bridge Builders Collective

Last Updated: [INSERT DATE]

This policy governs media submitted, created, preserved, or distributed through Bridge Builders systems.

---

# 1. Contributor Ownership

Contributors retain ownership of original media unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Media may include:

- photographs
- audio recordings
- oral histories
- videos
- written works
- archive materials

---

# 2. Usage Permissions

By submitting media, contributors may grant Bridge Builders permission to:

- preserve archive materials
- display approved content
- support educational initiatives
- promote stewardship programs
- maintain continuity systems

Specific permissions may vary by submission type.

---

# 3. Restricted Content

Sensitive or protected materials may receive:

- limited access controls
- family-only permissions
- steward review restrictions
- archival protections

---

# 4. Revocation Requests

Contributors may request review of previously granted permissions where operationally possible.

Certain public or archival uses may not be fully reversible.

---

# 5. Community Protection

Bridge Builders reserves the right to reject or remove media that:

- violates safety standards
- exploits participants
- contains unlawful material
- undermines archive integrity

---

# 6. Educational and Institutional Use

Approved materials may support:

- cultural preservation
- educational programs
- research initiatives
- continuity infrastructure development

Subject to governance review.

---
