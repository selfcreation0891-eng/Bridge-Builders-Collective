# Incident Response Protocol

## Bridge Builders Collective

Last Updated: [INSERT DATE]

This protocol defines operational procedures for safety incidents, moderation escalations, platform threats, and governance breaches.

---

# 1. Objectives

Incident response systems exist to:

- protect participants
- maintain emotional safety
- preserve operational continuity
- reduce harm
- support accountability

---

# 2. Incident Categories

Incidents may include:

- harassment
- exploitation
- platform misuse
- threats of violence
- unauthorized access
- harmful content
- governance violations
- accessibility failures

---

# 3. Initial Response

Stewards or administrators should:

1. document the incident
2. secure affected systems
3. reduce immediate harm
4. escalate where necessary
5. preserve evidence responsibly

---

# 4. Escalation Levels

## Level 1
Minor operational concern

## Level 2
Moderation or participant conflict

## Level 3
Serious safety or governance violation

## Level 4
Emergency or legal escalation

---

# 5. Investigation Procedures

Where appropriate:

- incidents may be reviewed
- access may be temporarily restricted
- moderation actions may be recorded
- participants may be contacted

---

# 6. Confidentiality

Incident information should only be shared with authorized personnel.

---

# 7. Documentation

Incident records may include:

- timestamps
- involved systems
- moderation actions
- communication logs
- resolution outcomes

---

# 8. Policy Review

Protocols may evolve as operational maturity increases.
