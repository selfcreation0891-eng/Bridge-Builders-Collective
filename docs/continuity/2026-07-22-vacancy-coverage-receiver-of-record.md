## What happened

Temporary vacancy coverage was adopted under the receiver-of-record model:
Maurice Jackson, founding steward, is the temporary receiver of record for
matters directed to the five vacant Permanent Steward Posts. Coverage state
for all five posts transitioned awaiting-human-decision →
temporarily-routed in `CURRENT_VACANCY_COVERAGE`
(`src/stewardship/vacancy-coverage.ts`), the operations registry, the post
status records, and the public status surface.

## Boundaries that hold

All five posts remain vacant, human-only, and in Observation-Only Mode.
Routing scope is receipt, acknowledgment, preservation, classification,
routing, handoff, escalation, agenda placement, and recording that decisions
remain pending — nothing more. The receiver may not approve, reject,
publish, merge, certify, appoint, remove, discipline, alter access, or close
a serious escalation unilaterally, and may not exercise any vacant post's
authority. SOPHIA may organize packets and may not be receiver, reviewer,
decision authority, or resolver.

## Review and recusal

Open vacancy-routed matters receive a documented review at least weekly;
missing a review changes nothing about an open matter. The routing
designation itself carries a coverage review date of 2026-10-20 and is
examined at each quarterly authority review. A concern involving the
receiver of record enters independent-human-review-required and is never
self-reviewed or self-closed; designating that independent reviewer remains
a pending human decision
(`docs/stewardship/decision-packets/INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER_DESIGNATION_DECISION_PACKET.md`,
DRAFT — HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED — NOT ADOPTED).

## Termination conditions

Per post: an adopted appointment decision for that post; or a later adopted
human decision changing or ending the routing; or suspension for conflict,
recusal, safety, or governance concerns. Appointment to one post ends
routing for that post only.

## Single-person dependency (named, not hidden)

The receiver of record and the pending-decision authority are the same
person. This concentration is temporary by design, bounded by the recusal
rule and the independent-review requirement, and is expected to shrink with
each appointment.
