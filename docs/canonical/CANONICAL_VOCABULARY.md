# Canonical Vocabulary

Version 1.0 — 2026-07-15. Authority level 5. Source authorities: Bridge Builders Constitution; adopted steward directive 2026-07-15; existing repository documents where cited.

Format per term: **Canonical spelling** — short definition / public-language definition / prohibited or misleading alternatives / related terms / source authority.

- **Bridge Builders Collective** — The whole ecosystem and its governing identity. Public: "a human-centered ecosystem supporting learning, regulation, stewardship, creativity, cultural continuity, shared knowledge, and meaningful participation." Prohibited: "BBC" in public copy (ambiguous), "Bridge-Builders-Collective" outside repository naming, "wellness app", "social platform", "startup". Related: stewardship, continuity. Source: Constitution §1.
- **stewardship** — Accountable, long-term care of people, archives, and systems, prioritized over authority and extraction. Public: "taking responsible, accountable care of what the community entrusts to us." Prohibited: "management", "moderation" (as synonyms), "admin power". Related: steward, continuity, governance. Source: Constitution §4; `doc/GOVERNANCE.md`.
- **steward** — A person with recorded stewardship responsibilities and accountability. Public: "a trained community caretaker responsible for safety and continuity." Prohibited: "moderator" (alone), "agent", "superuser". Related: stewardship, escalation. Source: `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`.
- **continuity** — Unbroken preservation of memory, operations, and relationships across time and generations. Public: "making sure what matters is not lost — across sessions, years, and generations." Prohibited: "retention" (metrics sense), "engagement". Related: Living Archive, preservation. Source: Constitution §1, §7.
- **contribution** — Material or effort offered to the ecosystem under consent and licensing terms. Public: "what you choose to share with the community, on your terms." Prohibited: "user-generated content" in public copy, "asset extraction". Related: consent, archive contribution. Source: `MEDIA_LICENSING_POLICY.md`.
- **participation** — Consent-based involvement in any environment or program. Public: "taking part — at the level you choose." Prohibited: "engagement" (metric framing), "conversion". Related: open participation, consent. Source: Constitution §11.
- **open participation** — Participation open to the public without invitation; does not confer authority. Public: "anyone may take part; authority is earned through stewardship, not access." Prohibited: "free-for-all", "unmoderated". Related: participation, authority boundaries. Source: Constitution §11–12.
- **Equal Creative Opportunity** — Principle and branch: equal access to creative means, not guaranteed equal outcomes. Public: "everyone gets a real chance to create." Prohibited: "equal outcomes". Related: Choice-Creating Circumstances. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Choice-Creating Circumstances** — Principle and branch: conditions engineered so people have real choices. Public: "building circumstances where genuine choices exist." Prohibited: rewording as "choice architecture" (dark-pattern connotation). Related: Equal Creative Opportunity. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Presence Principle** — Branch/principle: every presence is a gift; being present is itself contribution. Public: "showing up matters." Prohibited: attendance-metric framings. Related: participation. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Pattern Sync for Self Build** — Branch: pattern-recognition practice supporting self-construction. Public: "learning your own patterns to build yourself deliberately." Prohibited: "habit hacking", clinical/therapeutic claims. Related: Institute of Emotional Intelligence. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Perspectouence** — Branch: user-created term (canonical spelling exactly as written; never "corrected" to another word). Public definition pending steward articulation. Prohibited: respelling as "perspectuence", "persuasion", or any substitute. Related: Connective Dynamics. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Connective Dynamics** — Branch: the study/practice of how connections between people form and hold. Public: "how relationships actually connect and hold." Prohibited: "networking". Related: Relationship Stewardship. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Story Circle** — Branch: structured shared storytelling practice. Public: "a structured circle for telling and preserving stories." Prohibited: "content creation session". Related: Living Archive, cultural continuity. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **Living Archive** (The Living Archive) — Environment: intergenerational preservation system for stories, media, and memory under contributor ownership. Public: "a living, consent-based archive of community memory." Prohibited: "content library", "media vault", "data lake". Related: archive contribution, continuity. Source: `CLAUDE.md`, `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, steward directive.
- **System Rosetta Stone** — Environment: the canonical translation layer between ecosystem vocabulary, outside terminology, and implementation language. Public: "the shared dictionary that keeps every part of the ecosystem speaking the same language." Prohibited: "glossary" (understates authority), "wiki". Related: canonical vocabulary. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **BridgeBuilders Academy** — Environment: learning environment (canonical spelling: "BridgeBuilders" one word for the Academy name). Public: "where the ecosystem teaches — courses, practices, and orientation." Prohibited: "school" (institutional claim), "bootcamp". Related: programs, public knowledge. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **SOPHIA** — Environment: advisory AI presence, constitutionally bounded (Constitution §9). Public: "an advisory presence that supports stewards and participants; humans make the decisions." Prohibited: "AI steward", "autonomous agent", "the system's brain", any framing implying decision authority. Related: human authority boundaries. Source: Constitution §9; steward directive.
- **public knowledge** — Branch: knowledge the ecosystem maintains for open public benefit. Public: "what we know, shared openly." Prohibited: "content hub". Related: research, contribution. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **community stewardship** — Branch: stewardship practiced at community scale. Public: "communities caring for their own continuity." Related: stewardship, relationship stewardship. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **relationship stewardship** — Branch: stewardship of relationships as durable assets. Public: "tending relationships so they last." Related: Connective Dynamics. Source: steward directive 2026-07-15.
- **cultural continuity** — The preservation of cultural memory and practice across generations. Public: "keeping culture alive across generations." Prohibited: "heritage content". Related: Living Archive, Story Circle. Source: Constitution §10.
- **consent** — Explicit, revocable, documented, understandable agreement (Constitution §5). Public: "you decide, you can change your mind, and we keep the record straight." Prohibited: implied consent, "opt-out consent". Related: archive contribution, privacy. Source: `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.
- **archive contribution** — A consented submission to the Living Archive. Public: "adding your story or media to the archive, with permissions you control." Related: contribution, Living Archive. Source: `MEDIA_LICENSING_POLICY.md`.
- **public environment** — An environment whose status permits unrestricted public access with a published destination. Related: environment status standard. Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
- **pilot** (steward pilot) — A stage where stewards test an environment before wider access. Public: "being tested with stewards before opening further." Prohibited: "beta" (unbounded), "early access" (marketing framing). Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
- **preview** (public preview) — Publicly visible, explicitly incomplete. Public: "you can look now; it is still being built." Prohibited: presenting previews as finished. Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
- **in development** — Being built; not publicly usable. Prohibited: "coming soon" without basis, "launching soon". Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
- **internal** — For stewards/operations only; never appears in public navigation. Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
- **planned** — Adopted intention with no implementation yet. Prohibited: describing planned items as existing. Source: `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.

- **Permanent Steward Post** — An enduring institutional responsibility occupied by a trained and recorded human steward, bounded by delegated authority, accountable through records and review, and transferable through documented succession. The post is permanent; the individual occupying it is not. All five posts (Orientation, Continuity, Vocabulary, Product, Institutional) are human posts; SOPHIA advises all five and occupies none. Public: "a lasting responsibility of care, held by an accountable human." Prohibited: "admin role", "moderator position", "AI steward", any framing implying unrestricted authority or SOPHIA occupancy. Related: steward, stewardship, continuity. Source: `PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` (ratified 2026-07-20, adopted steward decision SD-2026-07-20-01).

Terms not listed here have no canonical status. New terms enter through `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`.
