# Post-Release Continuity Plan

## Weekly operating cycle

1. **Observe** — review the week's signals (list below).
2. **Record** — append findings to a dated continuity note in `docs/continuity/` (create on first use).
3. **Review** — check findings against the Constitution, status standard, and claims standard.
4. **Decide** — steward decides changes; consequential ones recorded per `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`.
5. **Update the registry** — statuses, pathways, and descriptions change only with evidence; CI must stay green.
6. **Improve one pathway** — ship exactly one participation/orientation improvement per cycle (stability before stimulation).
7. **Publish a continuity note** — short public note of what changed and why.

## What to track

Visitor understanding (can newcomers say what BBC is?), contact submissions (once B-EXT-7 closes),
program inquiries, participation-pathway failures, accessibility issues, broken links (CI link job),
unclear wording (Rosetta candidates), stewardship concerns, institutional interest, requested programs,
and trust questions.

## What NOT to track

No addictive-engagement metrics: no streaks, no session-time maximization, no notification response
rates, no dark-pattern funnels. Measurement follows the H-ROI framework's non-extractive principles.

## Standing priorities after v0.1.0

1. Close B-EXT-7 (open one real submission channel) — highest continuity value.
2. Complete C-007 placeholders (dates, contact, retention periods).
3. Human screen-reader audit.
4. Locate or decide against the referenced Lovable frontend (B-EXT-6); record either way in `docs/FRONT_DOOR_BOUNDARY.md`.
5. Review upstream PRs #1–#3 against the canonical authority order (C-009).
