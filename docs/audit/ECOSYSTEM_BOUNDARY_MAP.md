This audit used repository evidence plus the stewardship brief's ecosystem model to determine what belongs in this repository versus connected repositories.

| Capability | Current evidence in repository | Classification | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Boundary conclusion

The repository should remain focused on being the **umbrella public orientation and trust layer** for Bridge Builders Collective. It should explain ecosystem relationships, expose verified governance commitments, and route people to deeper systems without becoming a duplicate of Academy, Rosetta Stone, the Living Archive, or sector-specific deployments.
