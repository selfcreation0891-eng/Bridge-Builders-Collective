# H-ROI Metrics Framework

## Overview

H-ROI (Human Return on Investment) is the Bridge Builders Collective framework for measuring human-centered outcomes across participation, stewardship, continuity, and community wellbeing.

The purpose of H-ROI is to create measurable systems that evaluate:
- participant regulation
- engagement quality
- continuity
- retention
- trust
- stewardship effectiveness
- long-term resilience

The framework prioritizes measurable human stability and continuity over extraction-based engagement metrics.

---

# Core Measurement Categories

## 1. Regulation Metrics

Measures participant stability and interaction quality.

Potential indicators:
- session completion rates
- self-reported emotional regulation
- onboarding completion quality
- return participation
- interaction pacing
- accessibility success rates

---

## 2. Continuity Metrics

Measures long-term engagement and preservation.

Potential indicators:
- archive contribution consistency
- multi-session participation
- family continuity participation
- stewardship retention
- long-term platform engagement

---

## 3. Stewardship Metrics

Measures operational stewardship effectiveness.

Potential indicators:
- incident response time
- participant support completion
- onboarding success
- moderation resolution quality
- governance compliance
- escalation handling accuracy

---

## 4. Trust Metrics

Measures institutional and participant trust development.

Potential indicators:
- participant retention
- consent completion rates
- transparency engagement
- safety report resolution
- governance accessibility
- community feedback trends

---

## 5. Archive Integrity Metrics

Measures preservation and continuity quality.

Potential indicators:
- metadata completeness
- archive preservation stability
- upload integrity
- restoration success
- continuity indexing accuracy
- media accessibility quality

---

# H-ROI Design Principles

The H-ROI system is designed around:
- ethical measurement
- participant dignity
- transparency
- accessibility
- non-extractive analytics
- longitudinal continuity
- measurable resilience

---

# Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective of H-ROI is to establish a measurable framework capable of supporting:
- public health-oriented systems
- cultural continuity systems
- community resilience infrastructure
- stewardship evaluation
- archive preservation quality
- institutional partnership reporting
- evidence-informed operational development
