# Steward Eligibility Standard

Version 1.0 — Pending Ratification (activates with the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process)
Authority: subordinate to the Bridge Builders Constitution, `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`, and `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` (which sets baseline eligibility in §9.1; this standard elaborates it without weakening it).

Eligibility is not appointment. Meeting every requirement in this standard entitles a candidate to consideration, not to a post. Eligibility findings are made by human reviewers against evidence; they are never scores, rankings, or judgments of human worth.

## Shared eligibility requirements (all five posts)

A candidate must demonstrate:

### 1. Constitutional understanding

- understands the protected principles (Constitution §3);
- understands that human stewardship remains responsible for consequential decisions;
- understands the authority order (`ECOSYSTEM_AUTHORITY_ORDER.md`);
- understands Observation-Only Mode (`OBSERVATION_ONLY_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md`).

### 2. Evidence discipline

- can distinguish observation from interpretation;
- can distinguish recommendation from decision;
- records uncertainty honestly;
- does not make unsupported public claims (`PUBLIC_CLAIMS_STANDARD.md`).

### 3. Consent and confidentiality awareness

- understands revocable consent (Constitution §5);
- protects participant dignity;
- respects restricted information;
- understands archive and cultural-care boundaries (Constitution §7, §10).

### 4. Accessibility awareness

- understands cognitive, emotional, physical, and communication access (`ACCESSIBILITY_STANDARD.md`);
- can support accommodations;
- does not treat accessibility needs as incompetence.

### 5. Documentation ability

- can create understandable records;
- can maintain handoffs;
- can identify unresolved matters;
- can preserve context without unnecessary collection (`DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`).

### 6. Boundary awareness

- understands the post's permitted and prohibited actions (`STEWARD_POSTS_AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md`);
- understands when escalation is required (`STEWARD_HANDOFF_AND_ESCALATION_PROTOCOL.md`, `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`);
- understands recusal;
- understands that access is not authorization.

### 7. Relationship and communication readiness

- communicates respectfully;
- can receive correction;
- can preserve disagreement without retaliation;
- can work across roles.

### 8. Conflict disclosure

- discloses material personal, financial, organizational, relational, or decision conflicts (`STEWARD_CONFLICT_DISCLOSURE_TEMPLATE.md`);
- agrees to recuse where required.

### 9. Continuity responsibility

- agrees to maintain required records;
- agrees to provide a handoff;
- understands that the post must remain transferable (the post is permanent; the occupant is not).

### 10. SOPHIA boundary understanding

- understands that SOPHIA is advisory only (Constitution §9);
- will not present SOPHIA output as an adopted decision;
- will not delegate human authority to SOPHIA.

## What eligibility must not require

Eligibility must not require:

- a degree;
- a professional license;
- employment by Bridge Builders;
- technical credentials unrelated to the post;
- cultural conformity;
- a specific communication style;
- freedom from disability;
- prior institutional status;
- financial contribution;
- personal loyalty to an individual.

A credential requirement may be added only if an existing canonical authority explicitly requires it. As of this version, none does.

Demonstration is format-flexible: any orientation format and any accommodation recognized by the curriculum (`STEWARD_ORIENTATION_CURRICULUM.md`) is a valid way to demonstrate a requirement. Severe dyslexia, disability, communication difference, or accommodation use is never evidence of ineligibility.

## Open governance question — age, safeguarding, and legal capacity

This standard deliberately does not invent an age requirement. Whether age, safeguarding, or legal-capacity requirements apply to steward candidacy is an **open governance question**, recorded here and in the ratification packet, to be resolved by adopted steward decision — informed by applicable law and safeguarding practice — before any candidacy involving a minor or a person whose legal capacity is in question is opened. Until that decision exists, such candidacies are not opened.

## Role-specific eligibility

Role-specific criteria add to shared eligibility; they never weaken it.

### Orientation Steward

The candidate should demonstrate:

- ability to identify missing context;
- plain-language communication;
- cognitive and emotional accessibility awareness;
- ability to distinguish orientation from persuasion;
- ability to explain current, planned, preview, and inactive states truthfully (`ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`);
- ability to recognize when vocabulary or product design is causing confusion;
- respect for participant choice and pacing.

The candidate does not need to be a salesperson, recruiter, therapist, or marketing professional.

### Continuity Steward

The candidate should demonstrate:

- careful record stewardship;
- ability to preserve relationships and decision context;
- understanding of archive lineage and permissions;
- ability to identify unresolved commitments;
- ability to create complete succession handoffs (`STEWARD_SUCCESSION_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md`);
- respect for retention limits and confidentiality;
- ability to identify single-person dependencies.

The candidate must not treat unnecessary data retention as continuity.

### Vocabulary Steward

The candidate should demonstrate:

- careful use of canonical terms (`CANONICAL_VOCABULARY.md`);
- respect for protected user-created language (for example, **Perspectouence** — exactly as written, never "corrected");
- ability to identify vocabulary drift;
- ability to distinguish public, technical, institutional, and community language;
- ability to record proposed changes without silently changing canon;
- understanding of System Rosetta Stone;
- awareness of words implying unsupported authority, readiness, diagnosis, or institutional status.

The candidate may propose vocabulary but may not approve changes unless separately authorized under `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`.

### Product Steward

The candidate should demonstrate:

- ability to compare product behavior with canonical intent;
- accessibility and UX-coherence awareness;
- understanding of truthful environment statuses;
- ability to identify broken participation pathways;
- ability to read verification evidence (`docs/VERIFICATION_EVIDENCE.md` pattern);
- understanding that CI success does not replace human review;
- ability to work with technical contributors without claiming technical authority not granted by the post;
- awareness of manipulative or extractive design patterns (`CLAUDE.md` design rules).

The candidate is not required to be a software engineer; such a requirement exists only if separately adopted.

### Institutional Steward

The candidate should demonstrate:

- strong constitutional understanding;
- governance-record discipline;
- conflict-of-interest awareness;
- ability to preserve dissent;
- ability to distinguish review from approval;
- ability to route matters beyond delegated authority;
- understanding of appointment, removal, recusal, claims, and escalation;
- ability to avoid concentration of authority;
- understanding that the Institutional Steward cannot override the Constitution.

The Institutional Steward is always human. SOPHIA may never occupy this post.
