# Continuity Certification Standard

Version 1.0 — Active (observation-only) since ratification of the Permanent Steward Posts Charter, July 20, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-20-01)
Authority: subordinate to `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`.

Continuity certification is an internal Bridge Builders operational determination that sufficient records, handoffs, access boundaries, and succession readiness exist for a Permanent Steward Post to continue responsibly. It is not, and must never be described as, a governmental, academic, medical, legal, or external professional accreditation. It certifies the *post's continuity condition*, not the person's credentials.

## Certification criteria

A post may receive internal continuity certification only when:

- its responsibilities are understood;
- required records exist;
- open matters are visible;
- handoff pathways have been exercised;
- access is documented;
- authority boundaries are understood;
- SOPHIA's advisory boundary is understood;
- succession materials are usable;
- no critical unresolved constitutional conflict is concealed;
- review has been completed by authorized humans.

## Certification record

Every certification uses `docs/stewardship/templates/CONTINUITY_CERTIFICATION_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md` and must contain:

```text
Post
Review date
Evidence reviewed
Reviewer
Status
Conditions
Unresolved concerns
Expiration or next review date
Revocation conditions
```

## Allowed statuses

```text
Not reviewed
Observation-only
Conditionally continuity-ready
Continuity-ready
Suspended
Requires corrective review
```

Status meanings:

- **Not reviewed** — no certification review has occurred. This is the default state of every post at ratification.
- **Observation-only** — reviewed; the post is operating correctly within observation-only mode. This status describes continuity condition and does not itself expand authority.
- **Conditionally continuity-ready** — ready except for named conditions with dates.
- **Continuity-ready** — records, handoffs, access documentation, and succession materials are usable by a successor today. This status is an input to — never a substitute for — the adopted steward decision required to change a post's operating mode (`OBSERVATION_ONLY_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md`).
- **Suspended** — certification withdrawn pending review.
- **Requires corrective review** — identified failures must be corrected and re-reviewed.

## Roles

- The **Continuity Steward** coordinates certification review and assembles evidence. It may not issue certification for any post, including itself.
- An **authorized human reviewer** — designated per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md` — issues the determination. Certification of the Continuity Steward's own post is reviewed by the Institutional Steward or founding steward(s), never coordinated solely by itself.
- **SOPHIA** may prepare the evidence packet, compare records, and surface gaps. SOPHIA may not issue, deny, suspend, or revoke certification, and a SOPHIA-prepared packet carries the required advisory notice.

## Relationship to onboarding

When a post gains an occupant, the onboarding continuity record (`templates/STEWARD_ONBOARDING_CONTINUITY_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`, part of the appointment process, pending ratification) becomes part of the evidence this standard reviews. Appointment does not change a post's certification status; certification review continues to examine records, not occupancy.

## Revocation

Certification is revoked or suspended by an authorized human when its evidence no longer holds — records lapse, access becomes undocumented, succession materials go stale, or a concealed conflict surfaces. Revocation is recorded with the same care as issuance.
