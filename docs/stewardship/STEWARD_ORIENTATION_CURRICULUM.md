# Steward Orientation Curriculum

Version 1.0 — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01)
Authority: subordinate to the Bridge Builders Constitution, `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`, and `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ELIGIBILITY_ORIENTATION_AND_APPOINTMENT_PROCESS.md`.

Orientation completion is not appointment. Completing this curriculum makes a candidate ready for role-readiness review — nothing more. Orientation is gained, not delivered (Constitution §3): the goal is that the candidate genuinely understands, not that material was presented.

## Formats and accessibility

No single learning format is required. Orientation may be completed through any combination of:

- written material;
- audio;
- facilitated discussion;
- accessible summary;
- scenario walkthrough;
- demonstration;
- supported reading;
- other documented accommodation.

Severe dyslexia, disability, communication difference, or accommodation use must never be treated as lack of capability. The format used is recorded for continuity; it carries no weight in readiness review. Accommodation details are private candidate information (see the privacy section of `STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md`).

## Module structure

Every module records: purpose; learning outcomes; required source documents; practical exercise; evidence of completion; accessibility options; facilitator or reviewer responsibility; completion status. Completion status per module is one of: `not started`, `in progress`, `complete`, `complete with accommodation`, `waived by recorded human decision (reason)`.

Evidence of completion is reviewed by a human facilitator or reviewer. SOPHIA may prepare materials and questions but may not approve completion without human review.

## Shared modules

### Module 1 — Bridge Builders identity and constitutional purpose

Purpose: ground the candidate in what Bridge Builders is and is not.
Learning outcomes: can state the system identity and public-interest purpose in their own words; can explain "human regulation infrastructure" without hype.
Required sources: `BRIDGE_BUILDERS_CONSTITUTION.md` §1–§2; `CLAUDE.md` identity sections.
Practical exercise: explain Bridge Builders to a newcomer in plain language, truthfully distinguishing what exists now from what is planned.
Evidence of completion: recorded explanation reviewed by facilitator.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: confirm understanding, not recitation.
Completion status: —

### Module 2 — Protected principles and canonical authority order

Purpose: internalize the thirteen protected principles and how conflicts resolve.
Learning outcomes: can apply "stability before stimulation," "stewardship before authority," and "open participation does not mean unrestricted authority" to concrete cases; can walk the authority order top to bottom.
Required sources: Constitution §3; `ECOSYSTEM_AUTHORITY_ORDER.md`.
Practical exercise: given three conflicting documents, identify which governs and why.
Evidence: exercise record.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: verify the candidate resolves conflicts by authority, not preference.
Completion status: —

### Module 3 — Permanent Steward Posts Charter

Purpose: know the Charter as the candidate's operating frame.
Learning outcomes: can describe all five posts, shared responsibilities, the candidate's target post in detail, and the meaning of "the post is permanent; the individual occupying it is not."
Required sources: `PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`; `STEWARD_POSTS_AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md`.
Practical exercise: for five sample actions, state whether the target post may do them alone, with collaboration, with review, with founding-steward approval, or not at all.
Evidence: completed matrix exercise.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: check boundary accuracy.
Completion status: —

### Module 4 — Observation-Only Mode

Purpose: understand the current operating mode and its exit conditions.
Learning outcomes: can list allowed and prohibited activity; can explain why observation precedes authority; knows that mode changes require an adopted steward decision.
Required sources: `OBSERVATION_ONLY_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md`.
Practical exercise: identify, from six scenarios, which actions an observation-only post may take.
Evidence: exercise record.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: confirm no confusion between observing and executing.
Completion status: —

### Module 5 — Consent, privacy, archive protection, and cultural care

Purpose: carry the ecosystem's consent and preservation obligations.
Learning outcomes: understands explicit, revocable, documented consent; archive lineage and contributor ownership; cultural-care boundaries including restricted and family-only access.
Required sources: Constitution §5, §7, §10; `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`; `MEDIA_LICENSING_POLICY.md`; `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`.
Practical exercise: walk through a consent-withdrawal scenario end to end.
Evidence: scenario record.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: verify the candidate defaults to consent and restraint.
Completion status: —

### Module 6 — Accessibility and emotional safety

Purpose: make access a reflex, not a checklist.
Learning outcomes: understands cognitive, emotional, physical, and communication access; can support accommodations; never equates access needs with incompetence.
Required sources: `ACCESSIBILITY_STANDARD.md`; Constitution §6.
Practical exercise: review a sample surface or record for access barriers.
Evidence: review notes.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: confirm barriers found are real and framed with dignity.
Completion status: —

### Module 7 — Evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and decision

Purpose: build the four-way distinction that all steward records depend on.
Learning outcomes: can separate what was seen, what it might mean, what is proposed, and what was decided; records uncertainty honestly.
Required sources: `STEWARD_OBSERVATION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`; Charter §3.
Practical exercise: write a practice observation record from a supplied scenario.
Evidence: the practice record.
Accessibility options: any recognized format; records may be dictated or produced with support.
Facilitator responsibility: check the four categories are not blurred.
Completion status: —

### Module 8 — Documentation, continuity records, and handoffs

Purpose: make the candidate's work survivable by others.
Learning outcomes: can produce a usable handoff; can identify unresolved matters; understands succession readiness.
Required sources: `STEWARD_HANDOFF_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`; `STEWARD_SUCCESSION_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md`; `CONTINUITY_CERTIFICATION_STANDARD.md`; `docs/POST_RELEASE_CONTINUITY_PLAN.md`.
Practical exercise: complete a practice handoff record with deliberate gaps for the reviewer to find.
Evidence: the practice handoff.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: verify a stranger could act on the handoff.
Completion status: —

### Module 9 — Escalation, recusal, and conflicts of interest

Purpose: know when a matter is not the candidate's to handle.
Learning outcomes: knows the escalation routes and tiers; understands recusal triggers; can disclose conflicts without prompting.
Required sources: `STEWARD_HANDOFF_AND_ESCALATION_PROTOCOL.md`; `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`; `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md`; `STEWARD_CONFLICT_DISCLOSURE_TEMPLATE.md`.
Practical exercise: route four scenarios to the correct destination and urgency.
Evidence: routing exercise.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: verify safety matters route immediately.
Completion status: —

### Module 10 — SOPHIA advisory boundaries and responsible AI use

Purpose: keep human authority human.
Learning outcomes: can list what SOPHIA may and may not do; will never present SOPHIA output as a decision; understands the required advisory notice.
Required sources: Constitution §9; Charter "SOPHIA Advisory Relationship"; `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md` SOPHIA boundary.
Practical exercise: review a sample SOPHIA-prepared packet and identify what still requires human judgment.
Evidence: review notes.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: confirm the boundary is understood as constitutional, not stylistic.
Completion status: —

### Module 11 — Public claims, truthful status, and institutional language

Purpose: never let language outrun evidence.
Learning outcomes: knows the evidence classes; can distinguish documented / implemented / verified / deployed / piloted; recognizes prohibited claims.
Required sources: `PUBLIC_CLAIMS_STANDARD.md`; `ENVIRONMENT_STATUS_STANDARD.md`.
Practical exercise: correct three sample sentences that overstate readiness.
Evidence: corrected sentences with reasoning.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: check corrections preserve truth without adding hype in reverse.
Completion status: —

### Module 12 — Role-specific orientation

Purpose: deep orientation in the target post.
Learning outcomes: the role-specific eligibility criteria of `STEWARD_ELIGIBILITY_STANDARD.md` for the target post, demonstrated in context; the post's required records, handoff relationships, and review cadence.
Required sources: Charter §4 (target post subsection); role-relevant documents named there.
Practical exercise: role-specific exercise set by the facilitator from the post's real domain.
Evidence: exercise record.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: tailor to the post without adding un-adopted requirements.
Completion status: —

### Module 13 — Scenario-based readiness practice

Purpose: rehearse judgment before responsibility.
Required scenarios (all ten):

1. unclear participant orientation;
2. conflicting canonical vocabulary;
3. broken product pathway;
4. missing continuity context;
5. consent concern;
6. public claim overstating readiness;
7. conflict between two Steward Posts;
8. SOPHIA recommendation presented as a decision;
9. candidate or steward conflict of interest;
10. issue exceeding the post's authority.

Learning outcomes: in each scenario, the candidate observes, records, distinguishes, routes, and stops at the boundary of the post's authority.
Evidence: scenario records for all ten.
Accessibility options: scenarios may be walked verbally, in writing, or by demonstration.
Facilitator responsibility: confirm the candidate's instinct at the authority boundary is to stop and route, not to act.
Completion status: —

### Module 14 — Authority and access acknowledgment

Purpose: close orientation with explicit boundary acknowledgment.
Learning outcomes: the candidate can state, unprompted: what the post may do, what it may not do, what access they would and would not receive, and that access is not authorization.
Required sources: `STEWARD_ACCESS_AND_AUTHORITY_ACKNOWLEDGMENT_TEMPLATE.md` (reviewed, not signed — signing happens only at appointment).
Practical exercise: complete a practice acknowledgment.
Evidence: practice acknowledgment.
Accessibility options: any recognized format.
Facilitator responsibility: verify nothing in the acknowledgment surprises the candidate.
Completion status: —

## Completion

Orientation is complete when all fourteen modules are complete (or waived by recorded human decision with reason) and the facilitator records completion using `STEWARD_ORIENTATION_COMPLETION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`. Completion is an input to role-readiness review (`STEWARD_ROLE_READINESS_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`). It is not an appointment, does not create a right to appointment, and confers no authority.
