# Steward Role-Readiness Review Protocol

Version 1.0 — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01)
Authority: subordinate to the Bridge Builders Constitution, `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`, and `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ELIGIBILITY_ORIENTATION_AND_APPOINTMENT_PROCESS.md`.

Readiness is not authority, and a finding of readiness is not an appointment. The readiness review examines evidence, not personality preference: it asks whether the record shows a person prepared to carry a specific post's responsibilities, not whether reviewers like them, agree with them, or share their style.

## Reviewers

Readiness review is conducted by human reviewer(s) designated by the appointment decision authority (see `STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md`). A reviewer with a disclosed or discovered conflict regarding the candidate recuses. A candidate never reviews their own readiness. SOPHIA may organize the evidence packet; SOPHIA makes no readiness findings.

## Evidence reviewed

The review must examine:

- orientation completion (`STEWARD_ORIENTATION_COMPLETION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`);
- constitutional understanding;
- role-specific scenarios (curriculum Module 13);
- a documentation sample (practice observation record);
- a handoff sample (practice handoff record);
- escalation judgment (routing exercises);
- conflict disclosure (`STEWARD_CONFLICT_DISCLOSURE_TEMPLATE.md`);
- authority-boundary acknowledgment (curriculum Module 14 practice);
- accessibility and consent understanding;
- SOPHIA boundary understanding;
- continuity commitment;
- accommodations needed to perform the post responsibly — recorded as support the post provides, never as a deficit of the candidate.

## Allowed readiness findings

- Ready for appointment consideration
- Ready with documented support conditions
- Additional orientation recommended
- Additional scenario practice recommended
- Conflict review required
- Not currently ready
- Candidate withdrew
- Review incomplete

Findings are recorded with `STEWARD_ROLE_READINESS_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`. A finding of readiness is not an appointment; it makes the candidate available for a human appointment decision, nothing more.

## "Not currently ready" requirements

A finding of "not currently ready" must include:

- the evidence reviewed;
- the role-specific concern;
- possible preparation or support;
- review options (including when and how the candidate may return);
- confirmation that the finding is not a judgment of human worth.

Anonymous or unexplained rejection is not permitted. Every non-ready finding names its reviewer(s), states its evidence, and reaches the candidate in an accessible form. "Not currently ready" is a statement about preparation for a responsibility at a moment in time — nothing else.

## No ranking

Candidates are not ranked, scored against each other, or placed in competition. Where more than one candidate is ready for the same vacant post, the appointment decision authority decides between ready candidates as a recorded human judgment, with reasons, under the appointment protocol.
