# Observation-Only Operating Protocol

Version 1.0 — Active (observation-only) since ratification of the Permanent Steward Posts Charter, July 20, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-20-01)
Authority: subordinate to `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`.

Observation-only mode is the initial, mandatory operating state of all five Permanent Steward Posts after ratification. It is the operational form of "stewardship before authority" and "observation before action": the posts build records, relationships, and judgment before any of them holds expanded authority.

Observation is not authority. A post in observation-only mode carries full responsibility for noticing and preserving, and no authority to execute.

## Allowed activity

In observation-only mode, the five posts may:

- observe;
- review;
- document;
- classify;
- preserve context;
- identify risk;
- request information;
- draft recommendations;
- initiate documented handoffs;
- initiate escalation;
- prepare review packets;
- record unresolved questions.

## Not allowed

In observation-only mode, the five posts may not independently:

- publish;
- approve;
- reject;
- merge;
- deploy;
- alter participant access;
- change permissions;
- change canonical definitions;
- change public status;
- remove content;
- discipline anyone;
- appoint or remove a steward;
- certify themselves;
- close serious escalations;
- represent recommendations as decisions.

Nothing in this protocol restricts a person's independent duties under `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md` or `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`: safety escalation is always allowed and always urgent. The restriction is on unilateral consequential *execution* by the post, not on raising alarms.

## Transition out of observation-only mode

No fixed duration is defined. Time served does not end observation-only mode; an adopted human decision does. Transition for a post requires all of the following:

- recorded human review;
- evidence that required records are being maintained;
- successful handoff exercises;
- no unresolved constitutional conflict;
- completed role-specific orientation;
- continuity readiness (per `CONTINUITY_CERTIFICATION_STANDARD.md`);
- an adopted steward decision, recorded per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`, defining exactly what expanded authority the post gains.

The default state remains observation-only until such a decision exists. Expanded authority is defined per post and per decision — there is no general "graduation" that lifts all restrictions at once. Any authority not explicitly granted by an adopted decision remains withheld.

## SOPHIA in observation-only mode

SOPHIA's advisory boundary (Constitution §9; Charter, "SOPHIA Advisory Relationship") is unchanged by any mode. SOPHIA may help a post observe, compare, and draft. SOPHIA gains no authority when a post's mode changes, and a post may not route around its own observation-only limits by attributing an action to SOPHIA.
