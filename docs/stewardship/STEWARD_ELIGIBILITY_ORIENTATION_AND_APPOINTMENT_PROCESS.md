# Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process

Version 1.0 — Pending Ratification

Authority:
Subordinate to the Bridge Builders Constitution, Change Authority, and
Permanent Steward Posts Charter.

Operational state:
Inactive until ratified.

Purpose:
To establish an accessible, accountable, human-led process for determining
eligibility, completing orientation, reviewing readiness, making appointments,
and onboarding occupants of the five Permanent Steward Posts.

Controlling distinctions, applied throughout this process and its companion documents:

```text
Orientation completion is not appointment.
Eligibility is not appointment.
Readiness is not authority.
Nomination is not selection.
Recommendation is not approval.
Appointment to a post is not ownership of the post.
Appointment does not expand the authority of the post.
An occupant receives only the authority already assigned to the post.
Observation-Only Mode remains in force after appointment unless a separate adopted steward decision changes it.
The post is permanent. The person occupying the post is not.
```

## 1. Purpose

This process exists to:

- protect the integrity of the five Permanent Steward Posts;
- prepare people to carry stewardship responsibilities responsibly;
- prevent access from being mistaken for authority;
- prevent training completion from being mistaken for appointment;
- ensure appointments are based on evidence and recorded human judgment;
- provide equal creative opportunity without guaranteeing appointment;
- preserve accessibility, consent, dignity, and continuity;
- reduce single-person dependency;
- support intergenerational succession;
- prevent SOPHIA or any AI system from appointing or governing people.

The process evaluates readiness for a responsibility.

It does not determine a person's value, worth, identity, or human capability. A person who is not appointed, not currently ready, or not currently eligible has lost nothing of who they are. Every presence is a gift (Constitution §3); candidacy is one way of offering care, not a measure of a person.

## 2. Scope

This process applies to appointment of occupants for:

- Orientation Steward
- Continuity Steward
- Vocabulary Steward
- Product Steward
- Institutional Steward

This process does not:

- appoint people by itself;
- create employment;
- establish compensation;
- create professional licensing;
- establish governmental, medical, legal, or academic credentials;
- grant authority beyond the applicable post;
- authorize autonomous action;
- change Observation-Only Mode;
- replace removal, succession, or continuity procedures already established in the Charter (§9.3–9.6) and the continuity certification standard.

## 3. Candidate pathways

The following pathways into candidacy are recognized:

- **self-nomination** — a person offers themselves for a vacant post;
- **nomination by another participant or steward** — with the nominee's knowledge;
- **invitation to explore candidacy** — an invitation to consider, never an obligation;
- **succession candidacy** — preparation to succeed a serving steward per Charter §9.6;
- **interim-coverage candidacy** — preparation for interim designation per Charter §9.4;
- **community recommendation** — a community surfaces a person it trusts, with that person's consent.

Consent rules for every pathway:

- informed consent is required before a person becomes an active candidate (`docs/stewardship/templates/STEWARD_CANDIDATE_CONSENT_TEMPLATE.md`);
- no nomination may obligate a person to participate;
- a candidate may withdraw at any stage, without penalty and without explanation owed;
- no person may be appointed without knowingly accepting the responsibility (`STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_ACCEPTANCE_TEMPLATE.md`).

## 4. Process stages

The official stages, in order:

1. Vacancy confirmed
2. Nomination or application received
3. Candidate consent recorded
4. Eligibility review opened
5. Conflict disclosure completed
6. Orientation begun
7. Orientation completed
8. Role-readiness review
9. Appointment recommendation
10. Human appointment decision
11. Appointment acceptance
12. Access and authority acknowledgment
13. Observation-only onboarding
14. Continuity record opened
15. Role review scheduled

No stage may be skipped for an initial appointment. Each stage produces its own record (Phase templates); completing a stage confers exactly nothing beyond entry to the next stage. In particular: stage 7 (orientation completed) is not stage 10 (human appointment decision), and stage 10 is not effective until stage 11 (acceptance) is recorded.

Non-appointment outcomes — all of them ordinary, recorded without stigma:

- candidate withdrew;
- candidate declined;
- orientation incomplete;
- additional preparation recommended;
- not currently eligible;
- appointment deferred;
- appointment not approved;
- candidacy closed without adverse finding.

This process uses no competitive ranking language. Candidates are reviewed against the eligibility standard and role-readiness evidence, not against each other. An unsuccessful candidacy is never described as a personal failure, and records must not characterize it as one.

## 5. Privacy of candidate records

Candidate records are governed by the privacy section of `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md` ("Candidate privacy and record handling"), under `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md`. Candidate applications, accommodation details, conflict disclosures, and unsuccessful-candidacy records are never exposed through public routes. Public appointment records contain only what accountability requires.

## 6. SOPHIA's role

SOPHIA's support and its hard limits during this process are defined in the section "SOPHIA Support During Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment" of `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md`. In one line: SOPHIA may organize, summarize, compare, and draft for human reviewers; SOPHIA may not recruit, rank, score, decide eligibility, make readiness findings, approve, reject, appoint, grant access, or communicate outcomes before a human decision.

## 7. Ratification and amendment

This process takes effect only upon an adopted steward decision by founding steward(s) per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`. Until then it is inactive: no candidacy may be opened under it, no orientation completion recorded, no appointment made. Adoption of this process appoints no one; every future appointment requires its own recorded human appointment decision. Amendments follow `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md` and may not weaken the Charter, the Constitution's protected principles, or SOPHIA's advisory boundaries.
