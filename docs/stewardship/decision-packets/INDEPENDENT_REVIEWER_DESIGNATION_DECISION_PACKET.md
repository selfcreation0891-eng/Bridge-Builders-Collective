# Decision Packet — Independent Human Reviewer Designation

**DRAFT — HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED — NOT ADOPTED**

This packet prepares a decision; it makes none. No independent reviewer is
designated, and none is invented by code: matters that cannot be impartially
reviewed remain open in `independent-human-review-required` until this
decision exists. Prepared: 2026-07-22.
Decision authority: founding steward, per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`
(with the recusal caveat below).

## The question

Who serves as the independent human reviewer for matters the ordinary
authority structure cannot impartially review — concerns about the
Institutional Steward, concerns about the founding steward, and, since
SD-2026-07-22-02, concerns involving the temporary receiver of record
(currently the founding steward)?

## Why this matters now

With the founding steward serving as receiver of record for all five vacant
posts, any concern about the receiver has no internal impartial reviewer:
the escalation engine correctly parks such matters in
`independent-human-review-required`, where they stay open and visible but
unresolvable. That is the honest state — and it is also a standing gap a
human should close deliberately.

## Constraints from ratified canon

- The reviewer must be human. SOPHIA may organize the packet; it may never
  review, decide, or resolve.
- The reviewer must be independent of the matter under review — not the
  subject, not the origin, not someone whose own conduct is implicated.
- Designation is itself a recorded human decision. Where the founding
  steward is the subject of the matter that prompted designation, the
  decision record must document why the designation process itself remains
  fair (for example, designating a standing reviewer in advance of any
  live matter — the strongest option below).

## Options

1. **Standing independent reviewer, designated in advance.** One named,
   trusted human outside daily operations, designated now, before any live
   matter exists. Strongest impartiality; requires identifying that person.
2. **Per-matter designation.** An independent human is designated when a
   matter first requires one. No advance commitment; slower, and designation
   under pressure is weaker than designation in advance.
3. **External reviewer arrangement.** A person or body outside Bridge
   Builders entirely (advisor, peer-community steward). Maximum
   independence; requires relationship-building first.

## To adopt

Record the designation (reviewer as privacy-safe reference, scope, term,
recusal terms of their own) under `docs/stewardship/decisions/`; the
escalation engine's `independent-human-review-required` state then has a
named human route without any code change.
