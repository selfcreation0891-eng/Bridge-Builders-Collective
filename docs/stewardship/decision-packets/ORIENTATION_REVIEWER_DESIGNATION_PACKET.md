# Decision Packet — Orientation Facilitator and Readiness Reviewer Designation

**DRAFT — HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED — NOT ADOPTED**

This packet prepares a decision; it makes none. No facilitator or reviewer
is designated. Prepared: 2026-07-22.
Decision authority: founding steward, per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`.

## The question

The ratified appointment process requires two human functions before any
appointment: someone to facilitate orientation
(`STEWARD_ORIENTATION_CURRICULUM.md`) and someone to conduct the role
readiness review (`STEWARD_ROLE_READINESS_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`). Who serves
in each function for the first cohort?

## Constraints from ratified canon

- Both functions are human. SOPHIA may organize materials and prepare
  packets; it may not facilitate, assess, or find readiness.
- No person reviews their own readiness, and a facilitator's relationship to
  a candidate must be disclosed under the conflict disclosure template.
- A readiness finding is not an appointment; it feeds the separate human
  appointment decision.
- While these functions are undesignated, orientation and readiness stages
  simply cannot begin — the candidacy shell has no state that skips them.

## Options

1. **Founding steward serves both functions for the first cohort**, with
   recusal and a second human brought in wherever a conflict arises. Fewest
   people; concentration is real and should be named in the decision.
2. **Founding steward facilitates; an independent human reviews readiness.**
   Separates formation from assessment; requires identifying one additional
   trusted human.
3. **Defer until candidacy sequencing is adopted**, designating per-cohort.
   Cleanest pairing with option-by-option needs; delays readiness for any
   fast-emerging candidate.
