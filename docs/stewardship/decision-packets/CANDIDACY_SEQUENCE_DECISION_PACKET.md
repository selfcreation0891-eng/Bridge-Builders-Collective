# Decision Packet — Candidacy Sequencing

**DRAFT — HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED — NOT ADOPTED**

This packet prepares a decision; it makes none. No candidacy opens until a
recorded human decision opens one — and none can open before private storage
is designated (see the private storage packet). Prepared: 2026-07-22.
Decision authority: founding steward, per `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`.

## The question

When candidacies eventually open, do the five posts recruit together, in a
deliberate sequence, or as candidates emerge?

## Options

1. **All five together.** Fastest path to a full bench; heaviest simultaneous
   load on orientation, readiness review, and the (currently undesignated)
   facilitators; risks rushed reviews.
2. **Sequenced by dependency.** For example: Continuity first (so every later
   appointment is preserved well), then Institutional (so authority review
   has its post), then Orientation, Vocabulary, Product. Slowest full bench;
   each appointment strengthens the next.
3. **Opportunistic.** Open a candidacy when a plausible candidate exists for
   a post. Most responsive; least predictable; needs guardrails so no post is
   forgotten.

## Constraints that hold under every option

Per-candidacy consent before any active state; the ratified process's stages
without skipping; C-014-affected candidacies blocked until resolution;
multiple-post occupancy requires concentration review; appointment never
expands authority; every appointment carries acceptance and a review date.

## What this packet does not decide

Who candidates are, who facilitates orientation, who reviews readiness
(separate packet), or when — only the shape of the opening sequence.

## To adopt

Record the decision under `docs/stewardship/decisions/`; the sequencing
choice becomes an input to the appointment process's operational shell; no
code change is required to adopt (the shell accepts any human-recorded
sequence).
