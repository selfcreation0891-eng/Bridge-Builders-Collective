## Decision

While the five Permanent Steward Posts remain vacant, Maurice Jackson,
founding steward, is designated as the temporary receiver of record for
matters directed to:

1. Orientation Steward
2. Continuity Steward
3. Vocabulary Steward
4. Product Steward
5. Institutional Steward

This designation establishes a human routing and preservation responsibility
only. It does not appoint Maurice Jackson to any Permanent Steward Post. It
does not make any post occupied. It does not combine the five posts into the
founding-steward role. It does not grant the delegated authority, review
authority, certification authority, or approval authority of an occupied
Steward Post.

## The receiver of record may

Receive; acknowledge; preserve; classify; request clarification; identify
the appropriate human-review route; initiate handoff; initiate escalation;

that a decision remains pending.

## The receiver of record may not rely on this designation to

Approve or reject a recommendation; approve public claims; change canonical
vocabulary; merge or deploy product changes; publish archive material;
change contributor permissions; alter participant access; certify
continuity; determine candidate eligibility; make readiness findings;
appoint or remove a steward; discipline participants or contributors; close
serious escalations unilaterally; exercise a vacant post's authority.

## Operating mode

Observation-Only. This designation changes no post's operating mode.

## Review cadence

Open vacancy-routed matters must receive a documented review at least
weekly. The administrative meeting time is scheduled by humans separately
and is not invented by code. The absence of a meeting must not close,
approve, reject, or otherwise change an open matter. The routing designation
itself is reviewed no later than 2026-10-20 (recorded as the coverage
review date), and at each quarterly authority review.

## Termination

For each individual post, this temporary routing designation ends when:

- a human occupant is appointed to that post through an adopted appointment
  decision; or
- a later adopted human decision changes or ends the routing; or
- the designation is suspended because of conflict, recusal, safety, or
  governance concerns.

Termination is per post: appointment to one post ends routing for that post
only; the remaining vacant posts keep temporary routing.
