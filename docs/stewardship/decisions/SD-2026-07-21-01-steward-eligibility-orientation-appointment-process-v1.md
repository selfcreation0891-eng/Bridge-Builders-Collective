  receives no authority beyond the ratified Charter, authority matrix, and

  SOPHIA may support documentation and orientation but may not determine
  eligibility, make readiness findings, select, reject, appoint, grant access,
  or exercise governance authority.

  adopted to establish an accessible, accountable, consent-based, and human-led
  pathway for preparing and appointing future occupants of the five Permanent
  Steward Posts. The process protects the distinction between eligibility,
  orientation, readiness, recommendation, appointment, access, and authority.
  It ensures that no person receives stewardship authority without informed
  consent, role-specific preparation, evidence-based human review, a recorded
  appointment decision, acceptance of responsibility, and continuity
  onboarding. Ratification of this process appoints no person. All five posts
  remain vacant and continue in Observation-Only Mode until separate human
  appointment decisions are recorded.
