Decision: Adopted Steward Decision — Permanent Steward Posts Charter v1.

  The founding steward adopts the Permanent Steward Posts Charter v1 as a
  subordinate canonical governance standard of Bridge Builders Collective.

  The Charter establishes five permanent human stewardship posts:

  1. Orientation Steward
  2. Continuity Steward
  3. Vocabulary Steward
  4. Product Steward
  5. Institutional Steward

  SOPHIA remains advisory to all five posts and holds no independent approval,
  publication, disciplinary, appointment, certification, deployment, or
  governance authority.

  Upon activation, each post begins in observation-only mode. Expanded
  authority requires a separate recorded steward decision consistent with the
  Bridge Builders Constitution and Change Authority.

Rationale: The Permanent Steward Posts Charter is adopted to preserve informed
  orientation, canonical meaning, relationship and institutional continuity,
  product coherence, accountable human governance, and intergenerational
  succession. The Charter distributes enduring responsibilities across five
  defined human posts so that essential stewardship functions are not dependent
  upon one person, one application, one work session, or one generation.
  Observation-only activation is adopted as the initial operating condition so
  that evidence, context, handoffs, recommendations, and accountability records
  are established before any expanded authority is considered.
Scope: Adopts the Charter and activates the five posts as institutional responsibilities in observation-only mode, all vacant. Does not appoint any person; does not expand any authority; does not grant SOPHIA any decision-making power; does not begin a 30-day observation cycle; does not change public routes, environments, programs, permissions, or participant access. The vocabulary entry "Permanent Steward Post" becomes canonical with this adoption (resolves conflict C-011).
