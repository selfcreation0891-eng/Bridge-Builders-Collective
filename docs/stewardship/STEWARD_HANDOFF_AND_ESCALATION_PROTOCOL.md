# Steward Handoff and Escalation Protocol

Version 1.0 — Active (observation-only) since ratification of the Permanent Steward Posts Charter, July 20, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-20-01)
Authority: subordinate to `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`; escalation tiers remain governed by `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`; safety incidents remain governed by `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md`.

A handoff transfers a concern between posts with its context intact. An escalation moves a concern toward the authority able to decide it. Neither is a decision: escalation is not adjudication, and the receiving post gains no authority beyond its own mandate.

## Standard handoff packet

Every handoff must include:

```text
Record ID
Date and time
Originating post
Receiving post
Observed condition
Evidence
Known context
Missing context
People or systems affected
Consent or confidentiality considerations
Urgency
Recommended review
Authority required
Actions already taken
Actions explicitly not taken
Open questions
Resolution status
```

Use `docs/stewardship/templates/STEWARD_HANDOFF_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`. SOPHIA may prepare a handoff packet; a human steward initiates the handoff and remains accountable for it.

## Required handoff routes

These routes are the minimum. A route's existence never obligates a post to wait: urgent safety concerns follow `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md` tiers 3–4 and `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md` immediately.

### Orientation → Vocabulary

When unclear, conflicting, inaccessible, or undefined language is detected.

### Orientation → Product

When people cannot understand or complete a pathway because of interface structure or product behavior.

### Product → Orientation

When a technically functioning pathway still lacks sufficient human meaning or informed context.

### Product → Vocabulary

When interface labels, statuses, notices, or implementation language conflict with canon.

### Vocabulary → Institutional

When terminology implies unsupported authority, institutional status, clinical status, partnership, readiness, or governance power.

### Continuity → Institutional

When records reveal an unresolved governance decision, broken succession, authority conflict, or loss of required accountability.

### Any post → Continuity

When a decision, relationship, permission, rationale, or unresolved issue needs preservation across time.

### Any post → Institutional

When the issue involves:

- consequential authority;
- constitutional conflict;
- participant safety;
- consent;
- institutional claims;
- removal or appointment;
- serious governance failure;
- unresolved conflict between posts.

### Institutional → Founding Steward

When a matter exceeds delegated authority or concerns:

- constitutional interpretation;
- constitutional amendment;
- protected principles;
- ratification;
- final appointment or removal where reserved;
- unresolved authority conflict;
- major public institutional claim.

## Escalation rules

1. Escalation follows the routes above and the tiers in `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md`; the two are complementary — routes say *where*, tiers say *how urgently*.
2. The escalating post records what it observed, what it did not do, and what authority it believes is required.
3. The receiving post or authority — not the escalating post — determines the outcome. A post may not close its own serious escalation.
4. Concerns about the Institutional Steward route directly to founding steward(s).
5. Escalations touching participant safety activate `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md` without waiting for cross-post review.
6. Every escalation and its outcome are preserved as continuity records; disagreement is recorded, never concealed.
