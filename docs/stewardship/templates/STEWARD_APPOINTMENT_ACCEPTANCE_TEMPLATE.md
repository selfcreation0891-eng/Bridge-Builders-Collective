# Steward Appointment Acceptance

Template — Pending Ratification (activates with the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md`; `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` §2, §9.

No person may be appointed without knowingly accepting the responsibility. An appointment decision is not effective until this acceptance is recorded. Acceptance may be recorded in any accessible format.

```text
Appointee:
Post:
Appointment decision referenced:
Date:

Acceptance: (I knowingly accept this responsibility)
Post responsibilities: (I have read Charter §3 and my post's §4 subsection and accept these responsibilities)
Observation-Only Mode: (I understand I enter the post in Observation-Only Mode; expanded authority requires a separate adopted steward decision)
Prohibited actions: (I have read my post's prohibited actions and the authority matrix and will remain within them)
Record obligations: (I will maintain the post's required records)
Handoff obligations: (I will maintain a usable handoff and complete succession handoffs)
Conflict obligations: (I will disclose material conflicts and recuse where required)
Right to resign: (I may resign; I will provide a continuity handoff per Charter §9.3 where feasible)
Acknowledgment of non-ownership: (The post is permanent; I am not. I do not own the post, its records, its relationships, or its authority.)

Signature or recorded acknowledgment:
Date:
```
