# Steward Appointment Recommendation

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md`.

**Recommendation only — not an appointment decision.** This record carries no authority. It becomes meaningful only as an input to a separate human appointment decision by the recorded decision authority. Communicating this recommendation as an appointment is prohibited.

```text
Candidate:
Post:
Recommending reviewer(s): (named humans; an occupied Institutional Steward may recommend within delegated authority)
Date:
Readiness finding referenced:
Evidence summary:
Recommendation: (recommend appointment / recommend appointment with support conditions / recommend deferral / recommend against at this time)
Reasoning:
Conflicts and recusals in the recommending group:
Dissent within the recommending group: (preserved, never concealed)
Forwarded to decision authority: (date)
```

Field notes:

- SOPHIA may draft the evidence summary with the required advisory notice; the recommendation itself is human.
- A recommendation does not obligate the decision authority in any direction.
