# Steward Conflict Disclosure

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ELIGIBILITY_STANDARD.md` §8; `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` §3, §9. **Private candidate record — never exposed through public routes.**

Disclosure protects everyone, including the discloser. A disclosed conflict is a fact to be managed, usually by recusal — not an accusation and not automatically disqualifying. An undisclosed material conflict is a governance violation.

```text
Personal conflicts:
Family or relational conflicts:
Financial conflicts:
Organizational conflicts:
Decision conflicts: (matters the person previously decided, reviewed, or was subject to)
Prior disputes:
Proposed recusals:
Reviewer:
Review date:
Resolution: (recusals adopted / conditions / no material conflict found)
```

Field notes:

- "Material" means a reasonable person would question whether the conflict could influence judgment in the post's domain.
- SOPHIA may flag possible conflicts for human review; SOPHIA may not resolve conflicts of interest.
- The reviewer of a disclosure must not themselves hold an undisclosed conflict regarding the candidate.
