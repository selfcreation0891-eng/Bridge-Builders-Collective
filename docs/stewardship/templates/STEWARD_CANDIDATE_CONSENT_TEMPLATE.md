# Steward Candidate Consent

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ELIGIBILITY_ORIENTATION_AND_APPOINTMENT_PROCESS.md` §3; Constitution §5; `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

Consent must be informed, explicit, revocable, documented, and understandable. A person becomes an active candidate only when this record exists. **Private candidate record — never exposed through public routes.**

```text
Post:
Voluntary participation acknowledgment: (I choose to participate; no one may obligate me)
Right to withdraw: (I may withdraw at any stage, without penalty and without explanation owed)
Privacy notice: (what is collected, who may review it, where it is kept, how to request correction)
Record-use notice: (candidacy records are used only for this process and required governance evidence)
No-guarantee-of-appointment statement: (eligibility, orientation completion, and readiness do not guarantee or obligate appointment)
Candidate signature or recorded acknowledgment:
Date:
```

Field notes:

- Consent may be recorded in any accessible format (written, audio, supported acknowledgment), consistent with the orientation curriculum's accessibility commitments.
- Withdrawal of consent closes the candidacy as "candidate withdrew" — an ordinary outcome, recorded without stigma.
