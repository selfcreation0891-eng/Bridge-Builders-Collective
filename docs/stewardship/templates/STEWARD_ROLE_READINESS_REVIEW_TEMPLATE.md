# Steward Role-Readiness Review

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ROLE_READINESS_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`. **Private candidate record — never exposed through public routes.**

A finding of readiness is not an appointment. Findings are made by named human reviewers against evidence; anonymous or unexplained rejection is not permitted.

```text
Candidate:

Reviewer(s): (named humans; recusals noted)
Review date:

Evidence reviewed:
  Orientation completion record:
  Constitutional understanding:
  Role-specific scenarios (Module 13):
  Documentation sample:
  Handoff sample:
  Escalation judgment:
  Conflict disclosure:
  Authority-boundary acknowledgment (practice):
  Accessibility and consent understanding:
  SOPHIA boundary understanding:
  Continuity commitment:
  Accommodations needed to perform the post responsibly: (support the post provides; protected information)

Finding: (Ready for appointment consideration / Ready with documented support conditions /
          Additional orientation recommended / Additional scenario practice recommended /
          Conflict review required / Not currently ready / Candidate withdrew / Review incomplete)

If "Not currently ready":
  Evidence reviewed:
  Role-specific concern:
  Possible preparation or support:
  Review options: (when and how the candidate may return)
  Confirmation: This finding concerns preparation for a specific responsibility at this time.
                It is not a judgment of the candidate's value, worth, identity, or human capability.

Communicated to candidate: (date; accessible format used)
```
