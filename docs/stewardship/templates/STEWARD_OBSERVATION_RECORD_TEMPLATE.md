# Steward Observation Record

Template — Active since ratification of the Permanent Steward Posts Charter, July 20, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-20-01). Records created before that date exist only as drafts or test fixtures.
Authority: `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` §3; `docs/stewardship/OBSERVATION_ONLY_OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md`.

An observation record preserves what was seen, distinguishes evidence from interpretation, and states what human review is recommended. It is never itself a decision.

```text
Record ID:
Date:
Steward post:
Human steward:
Area observed:
Observed evidence:
Interpretation:
Uncertainty:
Canonical authorities checked:
People or systems affected:
Consent considerations:
Accessibility considerations:
Risk level:
Recommendation:
Handoff required:
Escalation required:
Decision status:
Follow-up date:
```

Field notes:

- **Observed evidence** — facts only: what was seen, where, when. No conclusions.
- **Interpretation** — the steward's reading of the evidence, clearly separated from it.
- **Uncertainty** — what is not known. Never convert uncertainty into false certainty.
- **Decision status** — one of: `no decision required`, `recommendation pending human review`, `handed off`, `escalated`, `decision recorded at <location>`. An observation record never records a decision inline; decisions use the Steward Decision Record template.
- If SOPHIA contributed analysis, mark those portions per the Charter's SOPHIA artifact requirements and include the required advisory notice.
