# Continuity Certification Review

Template — Pending Ratification. Records created before ratification may exist only as drafts or test fixtures.
Authority: `docs/stewardship/CONTINUITY_CERTIFICATION_STANDARD.md`; `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`.

Continuity certification is an **internal Bridge Builders operational determination** that sufficient records, handoffs, access boundaries, and succession readiness exist for a post to continue responsibly. It is not an external professional accreditation, license, or credential of any kind, and must never be presented as one.

The Continuity Steward coordinates the review. An authorized human reviewer issues the determination. A post may not certify itself. SOPHIA may prepare the evidence packet but may not issue certification.

```text
Post:
Review date:
Evidence reviewed:
Reviewer:
Status:
Conditions:
Unresolved concerns:
Expiration or next review date:
Revocation conditions:
```

Allowed statuses (from the certification standard):

```text
Not reviewed
Observation-only
Conditionally continuity-ready
Continuity-ready
Suspended
Requires corrective review
```

Field notes:

- **Evidence reviewed** — cite the actual records: observation records, handoff records, succession handoff, access inventory. Certification without evidence is prohibited.
- **Conditions** — for conditional statuses, list exactly what must change and by when.
- **Unresolved concerns** — no critical unresolved constitutional conflict may be concealed; if one exists, certification cannot issue.
- **Reviewer** — a named human with authority under the certification standard; never a post reviewing itself, never SOPHIA.
