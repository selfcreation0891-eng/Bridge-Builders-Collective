# Steward Orientation Completion Record

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_ORIENTATION_CURRICULUM.md`. **Private candidate record; accommodation details are protected.**

Orientation completion is not appointment. This record makes a candidate available for role-readiness review, nothing more.

```text
Candidate:
Post:
Modules completed: (1–14, per module status)
Format used: (recorded for continuity; carries no weight in review)
Accommodations: (protected information)
Facilitator or reviewer:
Scenarios completed: (all ten required scenarios, per Module 13)
Open questions:
Incomplete modules: (with plan, if any)
Completion date:
Readiness-review status: (not requested / requested / scheduled / complete)
```

Field notes:

- A human facilitator or reviewer records completion. SOPHIA may prepare materials but may not approve completion without human review.
- Format and accommodation use never appear in readiness reasoning as deficits.
