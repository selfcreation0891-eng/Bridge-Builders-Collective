# Steward Decision Record

Template — Pending Ratification. Records created before ratification may exist only as drafts or test fixtures.
Authority: `docs/canonical/CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`; `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md`.

**This record may only be used by a human with the required authority for the decision being recorded.** A decision recorded here without that authority is void and must be recorded as a conflict per `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md`. SOPHIA may draft options and organize evidence for a decision; SOPHIA may never be listed as the decision authority.

```text
Decision ID:
Date:
Decision authority:
Participants in review:
Issue:
Evidence reviewed:
Canonical authorities:
Options considered:
Conflicts disclosed:
Decision:
Rationale:
Scope:
Effective date:
Review date:
Dissent or reservations:
Implementation owner:
Continuity record location:
```

Field notes:

- **Decision authority** — the named human (or recorded steward ID) whose authority under `CHANGE_AUTHORITY.md` covers this decision. Constitutional matters: founding steward(s) only.
- **Options considered** — include the option of not acting.
- **Dissent or reservations** — dissent is preserved, never concealed (Charter §4.5 prohibited actions).
- **Scope** — what this decision does and does not cover. A decision grants exactly what it states; silence grants nothing.
