# Review Agenda — Template

> For the weekly observation review, monthly cross-post continuity review,
> or quarterly authority review (Charter §10). A meeting is not proof of
> continuity; this agenda exists to make the meeting earn its record.

- Review ID (e.g. `REV-CON-2026-07-27-0001`):
- Review type (weekly-observation / monthly-cross-post-continuity / quarterly-authority):
- Scheduled date:
- Convener (per Charter §10) and convener state (human-designated / vacant-post-awaiting-human-decision):
- Participating posts / humans (privacy-safe references):

## Conflict and recusal declarations (before substance)

Each participant declares conflicts touching agenda items, or states none.
Recusals use `STEWARD_RECUSAL_DECLARATION_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Carryforward (first, always)

Unresolved items from the previous review, by record ID. Nothing ages out
by being ignored.

## Queue summaries

Per-post: open counts, overdue counts, items awaiting human decision
(from `prepareQueueReviewPacket`).

## New items for review

Record IDs with one-line privacy-safe summaries.

## Decisions requested (not made by the agenda)

Questions that need recorded human authority, each pointing at its
recommendation packet.

## Required records

What must be produced by this review: outcome record
(`STEWARD_REVIEW_OUTCOME_TEMPLATE.md`), continuity ledger entries, next
review date.
