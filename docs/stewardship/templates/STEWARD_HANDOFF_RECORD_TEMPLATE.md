# Steward Handoff Record

Template — Pending Ratification. Records created before ratification may exist only as drafts or test fixtures.
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_HANDOFF_AND_ESCALATION_PROTOCOL.md`.

A handoff transfers a concern between posts with its context intact. The originating steward remains accountable for the completeness of this record; the receiving post gains no authority beyond its own mandate.

```text
Record ID:
Date and time:
Originating post:
Receiving post:
Observed condition:
Evidence:
Known context:
Missing context:
People or systems affected:
Consent or confidentiality considerations:
Urgency:
Recommended review:
Authority required:
Actions already taken:
Actions explicitly not taken:
Open questions:
Resolution status:
```

Field notes:

- **Urgency** — where participant safety is involved, follow `STEWARD_ESCALATION_MATRIX.md` tiers 3–4 and `INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md` immediately; do not let a handoff substitute for an urgent escalation.
- **Authority required** — name the authority the originating steward believes the matter needs (for example: Institutional Steward review, founding-steward decision). Naming it does not invoke it.
- **Actions explicitly not taken** — record restraint. What the post deliberately did not do is part of the evidence that authority boundaries held.
- **Resolution status** — one of: `open`, `received`, `under review`, `escalated further`, `resolved (decision recorded at <location>)`, `preserved (no action required)`.
