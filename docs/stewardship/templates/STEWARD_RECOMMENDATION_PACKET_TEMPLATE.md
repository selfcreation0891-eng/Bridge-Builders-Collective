# Recommendation Packet — Template

> A recommendation is not an approval, and this packet cannot become one.
> It prepares a question for recorded human authority. Decision language
> ("approved", "adopted", "appointed") is invalid inside it and is rejected
> by validation when the packet is mirrored as a typed record.

## Observed evidence

What was observed, factually, with references.

## Interpretation (labeled)

What the preparer thinks the evidence may mean.

## Uncertainty (preserved)

What remains unknown. Do not convert uncertainty into confidence to make the
recommendation tidier.

## Options for the human decision-maker

At least two where honestly possible, with expected consequences of each —
including the option of doing nothing.
