# Independent Human Review Required — Notice Template

> Used when a concern cannot be impartially reviewed inside the post
> structure — a concern about the Institutional Steward, about the founding
> steward, or any matter where every eligible reviewer is conflicted. The
> honest state is "this needs an independent human," and the matter waits
> there. Nothing invents an authority to resolve it faster.

- Why no internal impartial review exists (which reviewers are excluded and by what rule):

## What holds while this waits

- The matter remains open and visible in review packets; it cannot be closed
  by the origin post, the concerned post, or SOPHIA.
- Urgent safety elements, if any, remain escalatable at any time.
- All evidence is preserved with continuity ledger references.

## What resolution requires

A human independent of the concern — identified by recorded human decision
(who they are, why they are independent, what they are asked to review).
Their review and any resulting decision are recorded under
`docs/stewardship/decisions/` with full lineage.
