# Steward Access and Authority Acknowledgment

Template — Active since ratification of the Steward Eligibility, Orientation, and Appointment Process, July 21, 2026 (decision SD-2026-07-21-01).
Authority: `docs/stewardship/STEWARD_APPOINTMENT_AND_ONBOARDING_PROTOCOL.md` (access and onboarding section).

Access is not authority, and access is not authorization. This acknowledgment records exactly what an occupant can reach and exactly what that reach does not permit. It is completed after appointment and acceptance, at the access review.

```text
Occupant:
Post:
Appointment decision referenced:
Access review date:

Systems accessible: (each with: access owner, approval record, effective date, least-privilege scope)
Systems not accessible: (including, by default in Observation-Only Mode: production deployment,
  publication systems, participant-discipline controls, archive approval, permission management,
  constitutional editing)
Delegated authority: (exactly what the Charter, matrix, and any adopted decisions assign — nothing more)
Prohibited authority: (the post's prohibited actions; consequential actions requiring recorded human authority)
Least-privilege acknowledgment: (I hold the minimum access necessary; unneeded access is to be revoked)
SOPHIA boundary: (I will not delegate human authority to SOPHIA or present SOPHIA output as a decision)
Access-review date: (next scheduled review; revocation process referenced)

Signature or recorded acknowledgment:
Date:
```
