# Steward Succession Handoff

Template — Pending Ratification. Records created before ratification may exist only as drafts or test fixtures.
Authority: `docs/canonical/PERMANENT_STEWARD_POSTS_CHARTER.md` §9.3–9.6.

The post is permanent; the individual occupying it is not. This record is how a post survives the change of the person holding it — with relationships, open work, access boundaries, and context intact.

```text
Post:
Outgoing steward:
Incoming or interim steward:
Effective date:
Active responsibilities:
Open observations:
Open escalations:
Pending decisions:
Required relationships:
Protected or restricted information:
Access transfer status:
Records transferred:
Training completed:
Continuity risks:
Outgoing attestation:
Incoming acknowledgment:
Institutional review:
```

Field notes:

- **Protected or restricted information** — list categories and their consent/confidentiality boundaries; do not reproduce protected content in this record.
- **Access transfer status** — every access the outgoing steward held: transferred, revoked, or retained-with-reason. Access is not authorization; unneeded access is removed.
- **Pending decisions** — decisions awaiting an authority. Succession never silently closes them.
- **Outgoing attestation** — the outgoing steward attests the record is complete to their knowledge, including known gaps.
- **Incoming acknowledgment** — the incoming or interim steward acknowledges receipt and understanding, including the post's current operating mode (observation-only unless a recorded decision states otherwise).
- **Institutional review** — the Institutional Steward verifies the handoff followed the Charter; succession of the Institutional Steward post itself is reviewed by founding steward(s).
